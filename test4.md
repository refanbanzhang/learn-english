## 四、完形填空

阅读短文，从 A、B、C、D 中选择最佳答案。

Every year, a lot of waste is produced in cities. If it is not dealt with properly, the environment will be polluted. In many countries, new rules have been made to protect the earth.

In our school, rubbish is collected every afternoon. Paper, bottles and cans are separated by students. Then they are sent to a recycling center. Old books are given to younger students, and broken chairs are repaired by workers.

Last month, a "Clean Earth Day" was held in our city. Trees were planted along the streets, and posters were put up in public places. People were told to use fewer plastic bags. The event was organized by volunteers, and it was supported by the local government.

The writer believes that small actions can make a big difference. When waste is reduced and resources are reused, our planet will be protected for future generations.

1. If waste is not dealt with properly, the environment will be ______.
  A. improved  
   B. polluted  
   C. saved  
   D. visited

2. In our school, rubbish is ______ every afternoon.
  A. thrown away  
   B. collected  
   C. burned  
   D. hidden

3. Old books are given to ______ students.
  A. older  
   B. younger  
   C. foreign  
   D. tired

4. Last month, a "Clean Earth Day" was ______ in our city.
  A. cancelled  
   B. forgotten  
   C. held  
   D. refused

5. The event was supported by the ______.
  A. local government  
   B. supermarket  
   C. cinema  
   D. restaurant

## 五、阅读理解

阅读短文，选择正确答案。

The Great Wall is one of the most famous sights in China. It was built over many centuries to protect the country. Millions of workers were used to build it. Today, it is visited by tourists from all over the world.

In 1987, the Great Wall was listed as a World Heritage Site. It is regarded as a symbol of Chinese history and culture. However, some parts of the wall have been damaged by wind and rain. To protect it, new laws have been passed, and repairs are being carried out in some areas.

Many people think the Great Wall can be seen from space, but scientists say this is not true. Still, it is loved by Chinese people and admired by visitors. It is hoped that the wall will be protected well so that it can be enjoyed by more people in the future.

1. Why was the Great Wall built?
  A. To attract tourists  
   B. To protect the country  
   C. To store food  
   D. To hold sports meetings

2. When was the Great Wall listed as a World Heritage Site?
  A. In 1987  
   B. In 1997  
   C. In 2007  
   D. In 2017

3. What has damaged some parts of the wall?
  A. Tourists only  
   B. Wind and rain  
   C. Workers  
   D. Animals

4. What is being done to protect the Great Wall?
  A. It is being pulled down  
   B. Repairs are being carried out  
   C. It is being sold  
   D. It is being moved

5. What is the main idea of the passage?
  A. How to travel in China  
   B. The history and protection of the Great Wall
